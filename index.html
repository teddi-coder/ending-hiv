Channel roles& benchmarks

What each channel is for, how to evaluate it, and what numbers to expect. Reference this when reporting results or defending channel allocation decisions internally.

June 2026 Meta + Google Ads Trainer: Teddi Russell Attendee: Joel Evans
Channel roles
Google Search
Quality traffic

Captures people actively searching for HIV/PrEP information. Highest-quality traffic in the entire channel mix. Permanent fixture of every campaign — not experimental.

CTR 7–12% 2-Fun-1-1: 9.43%
Avg engagement 1:00+ 2-Fun-1-1: 1:02
Evaluate by CTR · engagement · impr. share
Google Display
Awareness

Reaches people who haven't searched — plants the brand and populates the retargeting list. Do not compare engagement time to Search. 6s is expected and fine.

CPC $0.10–0.20 2-Fun-1-1: $0.14
Avg engagement 5–10s expected for display
Evaluate by reach · impressions only
Meta
Community + CALD

Community targeting, CALD audience reach, video awareness. Instagram is 12× more effective than Facebook for 18–24 year olds. Separate placements at ad set level.

CPM (Reach) $6–14 health/community content
Frequency 1.5–3.0 cap at 2 / 7 days
Evaluate by reach · frequency · LPV rate
Grindr
Presence + political

Brand presence in a culturally relevant environment. Political and stakeholder value as much as performance value. Use Grindr for Equality (free) before committing paid spend.

CTR 0.8–1.5% awareness channel
Avg engagement 2–5s expected
Evaluate by impressions · awareness only
Sniffies
High-engagement

LGBTQ+-native, low ad saturation, inbox format drives genuine engagement. Outperformed all other dating-app channels in 2-Fun-1-1. Include from day one — not as a test.

CTR 3%+ 2-Fun-1-1: 3.37%
Inbox engagement 40s+ 2-Fun-1-1: 42s
Evaluate by CTR · inbox engagement
TikTok
Spark Ads only

Organic content boost only — Spark Ads to build view counts and social credibility on existing organic videos. Not evaluated as a traffic channel.

Format Spark Ads only
Evaluate by video views · social proof
Note not a traffic driver
Spotify
Reach extension

Extends reach of radio/audio creative. Value is cross-channel presence and frequency. Demonstrates presence beyond social platforms.

Users reached 376,014 2-Fun-1-1 result
Avg engagement 32s
Evaluate by reach · impressions only
